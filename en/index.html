Introduction

Hi! 👋 I joined the College of Computer and Information Science at Southwest University (SWU) in 2023, after earning my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS). In short, 🎓 PKUer -> ICTer -> SWUer.

My research lies at the intersection of Computational Social Science (CSS) and socially aware AI. I am broadly interested in complex social relations in networked environments, especially problems related to polarization, conflict, and social safety.

Along this line, I have long worked on signed social networks and their representation learning, with particular interests in modeling positive and negative social relations, dynamic social behavior analysis, and robust inference for downstream tasks. I also explore their applications in social computing scenarios such as recommender systems, peer review, and voting prediction. More recently, I have been extending this line of research toward social safety, social polarization, and social conflict, aiming to understand how risks emerge and propagate through complex relational structures.

In addition, my current research agenda increasingly focuses on LLM-driven social agents, especially for social simulation, human-agent interaction, collective behavior modeling, and broader social computing applications such as Education🏫 and Polymarket💰.

I welcome discussions and collaborations on social network analysis, polarization and conflict modeling, social simulation, and socially grounded AI.
